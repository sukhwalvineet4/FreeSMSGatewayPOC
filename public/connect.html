Please plug your Android mobile phone via USB cable.

How to Connect Any Android Phone (Samsung, Vivo, Redmi, OnePlus, Realme):

  • Step 1: Connect phone to laptop using USB data cable.
  • Step 2: Go to Phone Settings ➔ Developer Options ➔ Turn USB Debugging ON.
  • Step 3: Select USB Mode: File Transfer / MTP.
  • Step 4: Click the button above to auto-detect and pair your phone!
